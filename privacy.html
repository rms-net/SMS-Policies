RMS Privacy Policy: SMS Messaging Effective date: 2026/10/01 Information we collect. When you opt in to SMS, we collect your mobile phone number, your name (if provided), your consent record (date, time, and method), and message delivery and opt-out records. How we use it. We use this information only to send you the messages you requested, to respond to your requests, to maintain consent records, and to comply with legal and carrier requirements. No sharing of mobile information. We do not sell, rent, or share mobile opt-in data, mobile phone numbers, or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Limited disclosure may occur only to service providers that deliver messages on our behalf (such as our messaging platform and wireless carriers), who may use it solely to deliver our messages, or where required by law. Retention and security. We retain consent and message records only as long as needed for business and legal purposes and protect them with reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. Your choices.
